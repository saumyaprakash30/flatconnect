FlatConnect (“we”, “our” or “us”) is a residential society and apartment community management platform operated by Saumya Prakash, a sole proprietor based in India, trading as FlatConnect. This Privacy Policy is published in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made thereunder. It is intended to serve as the notice required when personal data is collected.

Please read it alongside our Terms of Service and Refund & Cancellation Policy.

2. Who decides what: your society and us

FlatConnect is software provided to a society, RWA or apartment association. That matters for privacy, because it determines who decides what happens to your information.

Your society is responsible for giving the notices and obtaining any consents it is required to obtain under applicable law for the information it records about its residents, staff and visitors. If your request concerns a society record — a dues entry, a residency record, a visitor log — we may need to involve your society’s managers to action it, and we will tell you if we do.

4. Subscription and billing data

Where a society subscribes to FlatConnect, we keep a commercial record of that subscription. This is our own record, kept for our own business, accounting and legal purposes.

4.1 What we record

  • Society name, city, flat or unit count, and the society’s identifier in our systems
  • Name, mobile number, email address and role of the society’s contact person or authorised manager, used for billing, quoting and support
  • Enquiry or lead information where the society first contacted us through our website, a demo request or a referral
  • Plan, subscription identifier, billing period, subscription start date and end date
  • Subscription status (draft, pending payment, active, suspended, cancelled, expired)
  • Whether the society is on a free period, a free trial or our small-society free tier, and the start and end dates of any trial or free period
  • The calculated price, any offer or discount applied, and the final agreed price
  • Commercial notes recording what was agreed — for example a negotiated rate or a promotional arrangement
  • Payments received: amount, date, method (UPI, bank transfer, cheque, cash or online payment), reference number such as a UTR or payment or order ID, and payment status
  • Outstanding balance and billing history across periods, including historical pricing
  • Refunds and cancellations, including the amount and the stated reason
  • Receipts and confirmations issued, and any tax information where we are required to collect it
  • An audit record of material administrative actions on a subscription — activation, price override, extension, suspension, cancellation, refund or complimentary access — including which of our administrators took the action, when, and why

4.2 Why we keep it

  • To provide and administer the subscription, and to give the society access to the features it has paid for
  • To issue receipts, answer billing queries and process refunds
  • To maintain our own accounting and tax records as required by law
  • To prevent and investigate fraud, and to resolve disputes
  • To contact the society about renewal of a subscription it already holds, or about the price applicable when a free period or free trial is ending

Subscription and billing records are kept for as long as required for accounting, tax, audit and limitation purposes — see section 15. We do not delete a financial record simply because an account was closed, because we are required to be able to account for money we received.

Residents do not see their society’s FlatConnect subscription or billing records, and societies do not see ours. Subscription records are accessible to our authorised administrators only.

5. Payment processing

5.1 Subscription payments to us

Subscription Fees may be paid by UPI, bank transfer, cheque, cash or an online payment gateway. Where you pay online, the payment is processed by a third-party payment provider — Razorpay Software Private Limited — under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive a transaction record (amount, date, status, order and payment identifiers, and the payer’s name or masked instrument details where the provider shares them). We do not receive or store your card number, CVV, bank credentials or UPI PIN.

5.2 Maintenance payments to your society

Where a society enables in-app maintenance collection, a resident’s payment may be made through a UPI intent or link that pays the society’s own account directly, or through a payment gateway. Where a gateway is used:

  • the payment is processed by the payment provider, and funds are settled by the provider to the society’s own verified bank account. The money does not pass through FlatConnect and we do not hold society funds;
  • we receive and store the transaction record — amount, date, status, order and payment identifiers, and the flat and resident the payment relates to — so that the society’s dues and receipts are accurate;
  • where a Platform Transaction Fee applies, we record the fee, applicable tax on it, and who bore it, as configured by the society;
  • society bank account and verification details provided for settlement are shared with the payment provider for that purpose.

Payment provider privacy terms apply in addition to this policy for those transactions. See Razorpay’s privacy policy.

Visitor information is not used for marketing, advertising, profiling, or any purpose unconnected with society security, and it is not sold or shared with anyone outside the society and our service providers.

6.2 Who can see visitor records

  • Residents can see visitor records relating to their own flat — their own pre-approvals, pending approvals and the visitor history for their unit. A resident cannot see another flat’s visitor records.
  • Managers can see visitor activity across their own society, because reviewing gate activity is part of managing the society. Managers cannot see other societies.
  • Guards can see what the gate workflow requires — expected and pending visitors and recent entry activity — within the limited role the society grants them.
  • FlatConnect staff do not browse visitor records. Our authorised personnel may access them only where necessary to operate, support, secure or repair the Platform, or where required by law.

6.3 Visitors: your information and your choices

If you visited a society that uses FlatConnect, the society recorded your visit — it is the society’s gate register, kept in our software.

  • You are not obliged to use the Platform. If you do not wish to provide information at the gate, tell the guard. The society applies its own entry policy, and that policy is the society’s decision, not ours.
  • To ask for correction or deletion of a visitor record about you, contact the society whose gate recorded it, or write to us at contact@flatconnect.in with the society name, the date of the visit and the flat you visited. We will locate the record and act on it, or route it to the society where the decision is the society’s to make, and we will tell you which. Some records may need to be retained where there is an ongoing security or legal matter.
  • We do not build a cross-society profile of visitors, and a visitor record in one society is not visible to another society.

6.4 Sensitive information

The visitor module is not designed to collect identity-document numbers, biometrics, health information or other sensitive categories, and societies should not use free-text fields to record them. Where a society nevertheless records such information, it does so on its own responsibility, and we do not require or encourage it.

6.5 What the Platform is not

Visitor management is a record-keeping and notification tool. It is not a security service, identity-verification service or surveillance system. We do not verify a visitor’s identity, and entry decisions are made by the society, its staff and its residents.

These are necessary to operate the Service and to deliver what your society has configured. They include:

8.3 Delivery channels

Delivering a message requires sharing the recipient’s contact detail and the message content with that provider. Delivery depends on those networks and on your own settings, and we cannot guarantee that any particular message is delivered or seen.

13. Data security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data we hold. These include:

  • Encryption in transit using HTTPS/TLS, and encryption at rest provided by our cloud infrastructure
  • Authentication by mobile number and one-time passcode, with secure session tokens
  • Role-based access control, so managers, residents, guards and staff see only what their role permits, scoped to their own society
  • Database access rules enforced at the infrastructure layer, not only in the app
  • Restricted administrative access, limited to authorised personnel and logged for material actions
  • Optional device-level App Lock (biometric or device passcode) for manager accounts, performed by your device’s operating system
  • Monitoring, patching and security updates, and use of reputable infrastructure and service providers
  • Account recovery tied to your verified mobile number, so that access cannot be transferred without control of that number

No system is perfectly secure. We do not claim that your data is completely or absolutely secure, and we cannot guarantee that the Platform will never suffer unauthorised access. Security also depends on you — keep your device secure, do not share your passcodes or guard credentials, and tell us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

We do not publish detailed information about our infrastructure configuration, because doing so would itself create a security risk. If you are a society evaluating FlatConnect and need more detail for your own due diligence, contact us.

14. Security incidents and breach notification

We maintain an internal process for handling security incidents: detecting and triaging the issue, investigating it, containing it, determining what data and which users are affected, recording what happened and what we did, taking corrective action, and making the notifications required by applicable law.

Where we become aware of a personal data breach, we will act promptly to contain and remedy it, and we will notify affected users and the relevant authority where and to the extent required by applicable law, including under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it. Our notification will describe, as far as we then know, what happened, what data was involved, what we are doing about it, and what you can do.

16. Account deletion

You may request deletion of your FlatConnect account at any time, either from within the App (Profile → Delete Account) or by submitting the form at flatconnect.in/delete-account.html. Both routes raise the same request.

When your request is processed:

  • Removed: your login access, your email address, your profile photo, personal documents you uploaded for your own reference, and your in-App preferences and notification settings.
  • Retained: your name and phone number, and society-related records — your residency history and the dues, payments and financial entries associated with your flat or unit. These form part of your society’s own records and are kept for society management, accounting and historical purposes. Your phone number is also retained so that, if you return, your account is reopened rather than duplicated.
  • Attribution: content you created within a society, such as notices and expense entries, remains with that society so its records stay complete.

Requests are typically processed within 30 days of submission. Deleting your account removes your access to the App; it does not cancel or waive any dues or other obligations you may owe your society. Documents you shared with your flat or with your society remain available to that flat or society.

If you are the only active manager of a society, you will be asked to add another manager before your deletion request can be submitted. This ensures the society is not left without anyone able to administer it.

If you sign in again later using the same phone number, your account is reopened and your society records remain linked to it. The personal information removed during deletion is not restored, and any management role you held is not reinstated.

Deleting an individual account does not end a society’s subscription. A society that wants to close its account should contact us — see section 15.1 and the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

17.1 How to exercise them

Write to contact@flatconnect.in from the email address or using the mobile number registered to your account, or contact the Grievance Officer in section 24. Tell us what you want and enough detail to identify your record — your name, your society, and your flat.

We will acknowledge within 48 hours and respond within 30 days, or sooner where a shorter period is prescribed by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before acting, which protects you. We will tell you if we cannot fully meet a request, and why.

17.2 Where a request concerns a society record
