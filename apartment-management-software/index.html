Who is FlatConnect apartment management software for?

FlatConnect is purpose-built for small and mid-sized housing societies, apartment complexes and gated communities in India — typically 8 to a few hundred flats. It's ideal for managing committees, secretaries and treasurers who are tired of juggling diaries, spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.

Whether you're a self-managed society looking to go digital for the first time, or a community switching away from expensive software that's overkill for your size, FlatConnect scales to fit — starting free and staying affordable as you grow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about FlatConnect's apartment management software.

Is FlatConnect apartment management software free?

Yes. Every FlatConnect feature is completely free for all of 2026, so your society can use everything at no cost right now. After 2026, FlatConnect continues on an affordable base plan with optional premium features for advanced needs.

Does it work for small societies with few flats?

Absolutely. FlatConnect is designed specifically for small and mid-sized societies with 8+ flats, unlike enterprise platforms built for large townships. There's no minimum flat requirement to get real value.

Can residents pay maintenance through the app?

Yes. Residents pay maintenance directly via UPI, and the app tracks payment status automatically and sends reminders for pending dues — improving collection rates significantly.

Is there an iPhone version?

FlatConnect is available as an Android app on Google Play and as a web app at app.flatconnect.in that works on iPhone, iPad and any modern browser with the same features.

How long does it take to set up?

Most societies are up and running within a day. You create your society profile, import residents and opening dues, and start collecting — with support available on WhatsApp throughout.
