4. Description of the Service

FlatConnect is a software platform for residential societies and gated communities. Depending on the features enabled for a Society, the Service may provide:

  • Maintenance billing, dues tracking and collection records
  • Expense logging, income records and society accounting reports
  • Payment reminders and notifications by in-app message, email and WhatsApp

8. Visitor management and gate security

Where a Society has enabled visitor management, the Platform allows Guards to record visitors at the gate, Residents to pre-approve expected visitors, guests, deliveries and cabs, and Managers to review their Society’s visitor activity.

8.1 Purpose and responsibility

  • Visitor information may be collected and used only for premises security, access control, and the Society’s own records of who entered and left. It must not be used for marketing, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.
  • The Society is responsible for the visitor information recorded in its account, including information entered by its Guards, for limiting collection to what is necessary, and for the conduct of its gate staff. FlatConnect provides the recording and retrieval functionality; we do not decide what a Society collects at its gate.
  • The Society must ensure that a notice of what is being recorded and why is available at the gate or otherwise made available to visitors, as required by applicable law.
  • A visitor is not obliged to use the Platform. Where a visitor declines to provide information, the Society may apply its own entry policy, but the Society remains responsible for ensuring that policy is lawful and reasonable.

8.2 Who can see what

  • Residents can see visitor records relating to their own flat, including their own pre-approvals and the visitors recorded as visiting their flat.
  • Managers can see visitor activity across the Society, because that is the function they perform.
  • Guards can see what the gate workflow requires — expected and pending visitors, and recent entry activity — and act within the limited role the Society grants them.

Retention of visitor records, and how a visitor can ask for correction or deletion of their information, are described in our Privacy Policy.

8.3 Security limitations

FlatConnect is a record-keeping and notification tool, not a security service. We do not provide guarding, physical access control, surveillance, verification of visitor identity, or emergency response. We do not verify that a person is who they say they are at the gate. Decisions about whether to allow entry are made by the Society, its staff and its residents. We are not responsible for incidents arising from entry or refusal of entry, from information recorded incorrectly, from a guard not recording an entry, or from a device or network being unavailable at the gate. Societies should not rely on the Platform as their only security measure.
