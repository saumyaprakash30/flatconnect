Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about maintenance collection with FlatConnect.

How does UPI maintenance collection work?

Residents receive their maintenance bill in the app and pay via any UPI app in a few taps. Funds move directly bank-to-bank — FlatConnect never holds your money — and the payment is recorded and receipted automatically.

Will this really improve our collection rate?

Yes. Societies typically see collection rates rise from around 60-75% to over 90% after switching, because paying is effortless and automated reminders remove the need for manual follow-up.

Can we charge late fees automatically?

Yes. You can configure late fee rules once, and the app applies them consistently to overdue accounts — no manual tracking or awkward conversations required.

Do residents get payment receipts?

Every payment generates a digital receipt automatically, giving residents proof of payment and the committee a fully transparent, audit-ready record.

Is it suitable for a small society?

Definitely. FlatConnect is built for small and mid-sized societies and is completely free for all of 2026, then continues on an affordable base plan with optional premium features.
