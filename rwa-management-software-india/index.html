Frequently asked questions

Common questions about FlatConnect for Resident Welfare Associations.

What is RWA management software?

RWA management software is a digital tool that helps a Resident Welfare Association manage its members, collect maintenance, send notices, keep important documents and maintain transparent accounts — replacing diaries, spreadsheets and scattered WhatsApp groups.

Is FlatConnect suitable for Indian RWAs?

Yes. FlatConnect is built specifically for Indian communities, with UPI-based collection, a simple interface elderly residents can use, notices and broadcasts for festivals and meetings, and a Society Library for bylaws and AGM minutes.

How does it improve transparency at the AGM?

All income and expenses are logged in real time, so you can present clean, audit-ready financial reports at the AGM instead of scrambling to reconcile records — which builds member trust.

Can we keep bylaws and AGM minutes in the app?

Yes. The Society Library keeps bylaws, AGM minutes, insurance papers and other important documents in one place. You choose whether each document is visible to all residents or to the committee only, and get reminders before documents like insurance expire.

How much does it cost for an RWA?

FlatConnect is completely free for all of 2026, then continues on an affordable base plan with optional premium features — far more affordable than traditional enterprise association software.
