Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about FlatConnect for Resident Welfare Associations.

What is RWA management software?

RWA management software is a digital tool that helps a Resident Welfare Association manage its members, collect maintenance, track complaints, send notices and maintain transparent accounts — replacing diaries, spreadsheets and scattered WhatsApp groups.

Is FlatConnect suitable for Indian RWAs?

Yes. FlatConnect is built specifically for Indian communities, with UPI-based collection, a simple interface elderly residents can use, complaint tracking, and event coordination for festivals and meetings.

How does it improve transparency at the AGM?

All income and expenses are logged in real time, so you can present clean, audit-ready financial reports at the AGM instead of scrambling to reconcile records — which builds member trust.

Can members raise and track complaints?

Yes. Members can log complaints in the app, and the committee can assign and resolve them with a clear status trail, so nothing slips through the cracks.

How much does it cost for an RWA?

FlatConnect is completely free for all of 2026, then continues on an affordable base plan with optional premium features — far more affordable than traditional enterprise association software.
