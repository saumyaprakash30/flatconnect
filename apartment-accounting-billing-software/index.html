Frequently asked questions

Common questions about society accounting and billing with FlatConnect.

Do I need accounting knowledge to use it?

No. FlatConnect is designed for society treasurers, not accountants. Billing is automated and expenses are logged with simple categories, so you get professional reports without professional training.

Can it generate reports for our AGM and audit?

Yes. FlatConnect produces monthly and annual financial reports with a full, traceable transaction history — so your accounts are audit-ready and AGM-ready at any time.

Does it handle both billing and payments?

Yes. It automatically bills every flat, collects payments via UPI, issues receipts, and records the transaction in your accounts — billing and accounting stay in sync automatically.

Is our financial data safe?

Your records are stored securely in the cloud and backed up, so you're not relying on a single Excel file on one person's laptop. The committee can access records with appropriate permissions.

How much does the accounting software cost?

FlatConnect is completely free for all of 2026, then continues on an affordable base plan with optional premium features — a fraction of the cost of traditional accounting or ERP software.
