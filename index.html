One app for the whole society

Built around the people who actually run a society: the treasurer, the secretary, the gate and every resident.

For the treasurer

Collect maintenance and keep accounts the committee can trust

  • Maintenance bills from a plan, on monthly or quarterly cycles
  • Dues tracking with reminders, receipts and late fees
  • Online payments in the app, plus cash and bank payments recorded for your confirmation
  • The society's payment QR printed on bills and invoices
  • Bank and cash accounts with current balances, opening balances and month-end reconciliation
  • Advances adjusted against future dues, and corrections kept with full history
  • Balance sheet, ledger and PDF reports ready for the AGM

For the secretary

Keep residents informed and records in order.

  • Notices with attachments, and broadcasts to the whole society
  • Society Library for bylaws, AGM minutes and insurance, shared with everyone or the committee only
  • Reminders before documents need renewal
  • Towers, flats and commercial properties in one directory
  • Owner and tenant records, with move-out history kept
  • Vehicles with RFID stickers and parking slots

At the gate

Know who comes in, and let residents decide.

  • Entry and exit log with timestamps
  • Visitor photos sent to residents for approval
  • Cab visitors with vehicle numbers
  • Multiple gates, with guard access by code and PIN
  • Pre-approved guests and deliveries
  • A registry of maids, cooks and drivers, verified by the manager

For residents

Everything about their home, on their phone.

  • See dues and pay online, with instant receipts
  • Report a cash or bank payment from the app
  • Approve visitors from anywhere
  • New notices highlighted until they're read
  • Link their domestic help to their flat
  • Bylaws and society documents always at hand

Frequently asked questions

Is FlatConnect available as an app?

Yes! FlatConnect is available as an Android app on Google Play Store and as a web app for iOS/browser users. You can download the Android app or access the web version at app.flatconnect.in.

Can iPhone users use FlatConnect?

Absolutely! iPhone users can access FlatConnect through our web app at app.flatconnect.in. It works perfectly on Safari and all modern browsers, providing the same features as the Android app.

Is the FlatConnect app free to download?

Yes, FlatConnect is completely free to download from Google Play Store. The web app is also free to use. We offer affordable subscription plans for societies, not individual users.

What features are available in FlatConnect?

FlatConnect covers maintenance billing and dues, society accounts (income, expenses, bank and cash balances, balance sheet), online and offline payment collection, residents and commercial properties, visitor and gate management, a domestic-help registry, notices and broadcasts, a Society Library for important documents, CSV import, and roles and permissions for committee members.

Which features are free to use?

Every FlatConnect feature is completely free for all of 2026, so your society can use everything at no cost right now. Societies that join after 2026 start with a 3-month free trial, then continue on an affordable base plan with optional premium features for advanced needs.

Can residents pay maintenance online?

Yes. Residents can pay their dues online in the app and get a receipt. If they pay by cash or bank transfer, they can record the payment in the app for the manager to confirm. Bills and invoices can also carry the society's payment QR code.
